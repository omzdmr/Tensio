# Tensio Market & User Research Log

Purpose: capture recurring user needs, complaints, competitor patterns and product opportunities. This is evidence for product decisions, not a feature dumping ground.

## Research rules
- Date every entry.
- Link/identify sources when possible.
- Separate repeated patterns from one-off opinions.
- Record what users dislike as carefully as what they request.
- Include iOS App Store, Google Play, Reddit, health communities, Turkish forums such as Ekşi Sözlük where relevant, and competitor help/review pages.
- Do not copy competitor text/design.
- Avoid treating anecdotal comments as medical evidence.
- For medical/safety behavior use clinical/official references, not app-store reviews.

## Initial hypotheses to validate
- Fast entry is more important than dashboard complexity.
- Users value PDF/doctor sharing.
- Reminder flexibility matters.
- Repeated-reading/session support is under-served.
- People dislike subscriptions that lock basic history/export.
- Import/export and device sync failures cause disproportionate frustration.
- Older users benefit from large typography and simplified capture.
- Family/caregiver tracking may be valuable if profiles cannot be confused.

These are hypotheses, not facts. Research automation should validate or reject them.
